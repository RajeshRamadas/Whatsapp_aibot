Sample Beauty Salon reference for a disposable accuracy workspace.

Services and durations
A haircut takes 45 minutes. A blow-dry takes 30 minutes. Styling
consultations are complimentary.

Opening hours
Monday is closed. Tuesday through Saturday the salon opens at 10 a.m.
Sunday opening is at 11 a.m.

Appointments
An appointment can be rescheduled up to 24 hours before the start time.
Booking is confirmed only when an appointment confirmation is issued.

AI look previews
Guests must give explicit consent before a photo is used for an AI look preview.
Previews are illustrative and do not guarantee the final result. A stylist
can explain what is achievable during a consultation.
